Discover Your Major

Data science combines programming, mathematics and statistics to understand information and discover useful patterns. Students learn how to organize, analyze and interpret data.

Artificial intelligence explores how computers can perform tasks such as recognizing patterns, making predictions and solving problems. These skills support applications in education, business and many other fields.
