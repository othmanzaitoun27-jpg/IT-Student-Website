Our Mission

IT Course Explorer is a student project at Applied Science Private University. Our goal is to help first-year IT students explore different IT majors and understand the courses and topics they will study before starting their university journey.

Our Team

We are IT students at Applied Science Private University. We created this website to help students explore IT majors and understand their academic options.
