Discover Your Major

Digital Reality is a field of technology that focuses on creating interactive digital experiences using technologies such as Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR). These technologies allow users to explore virtual environments, interact with digital objects, and experience computer-generated worlds. Digital Reality is used in different fields, including education, entertainment, healthcare, and training.

Game Development is the process of designing, programming, and creating video games. It involves several stages, including developing game ideas, designing characters and environments, programming game mechanics, testing, and improving the final product. Students interested in this field learn programming, problem-solving, creativity, and digital design skills.

Digital Reality and Game Development are closely connected because both focus on creating interactive experiences. They offer career opportunities such as Game Developer, Game Designer, VR Developer, AR Developer, and 3D Artist. These fields continue to grow as technology advances and the demand for interactive digital experiences increases.

Technologies Used

  • Virtual Reality (VR)
  • Augmented Reality (AR)
  • 3D Modeling and Design
  • Game Programming
  • Interactive Digital Environments

Career Opportunities

  • Game Developer
  • Game Designer
  • Virtual Reality Developer
  • Augmented Reality Developer
  • 3D Artist
